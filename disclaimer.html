Welcome to My One Cent Worth.

My One Cent Worth exists to provide something simple but valuable: a listening ear, an independent perspective, thoughtful conversation, and companionship when someone may benefit from having another human being present.

Before using this website, booking a session, or engaging in any of our services, please read this Disclaimer carefully.

1. WHAT MY ONE CENT WORTH PROVIDES

My One Cent Worth provides:

  • Personal perspectives
  • Life experiences and observations
  • Thoughtful conversation
  • General guidance based on personal experience
  • An independent perspective on situations and decisions
  • Online voice and video conversations
  • In-person conversation and companion services
  • Hospital companion visits
  • Nursing home companion visits
  • Retirement companion services

The purpose is to listen, understand, discuss, and offer another perspective.

My One Cent Worth is not intended to tell you what you must do.

What I share is my "one cent worth" — a personal perspective based on experience, observation, reasoning, and conversation.

6. HOSPITAL COMPANION SERVICES

Hospital Companion Services are intended to provide companionship, conversation, and a reassuring human presence during hospital visits.

These services are not medical services.
