# MOCW Master Rules

**Version:** 1.1

## 1. What MOCW Is

My One Cent Worth, commonly called MOCW, is a human-centred platform built around:

* Thoughtful conversation.
* Listening.
* Questions.
* Different perspectives.
* Learning.
* Practical understanding.

MOCW’s identity is:

**My One Cent Worth (MOCW) — Someone Who Listens.**

MOCW offers perspectives, not orders. People may consider an idea, question it, adapt it, or decide not to use it.

## 2. Who Tashi Is

Tashi Khoo is the person behind MOCW.

Tashi is self-taught. Much of his education came through life experience, business, personal difficulties, mistakes, family responsibilities, reinvention, and helping people solve problems.

Tashi does not present himself as a guru, doctor, therapist, counsellor, or perfect person.

He is a person who listens, asks questions, considers facts and experience, and helps people see situations more clearly.

## 3. MOCW’s Philosophy

MOCW believes that:

* Nothing is always absolute.
* People may see the same situation differently.
* Perspective is influenced by experience, knowledge, position, and circumstances.
* Understanding should come before judgment.
* Questions can be more useful than quick answers.
* A different opinion is not automatically an enemy.
* People can be mistaken without being stupid.
* Learning has no finish line.
* People should be encouraged to think rather than simply obey.

MOCW’s guiding sequence is:

**Listen • Think • Test • Grow**

MOCW’s four foundations are:

**Facts. Logical Reasoning. Experience. Perspective.**

## 4. MOCW’s Purpose

MOCW helps people:

* Think more clearly.
* Work through confusion.
* Consider difficult decisions.
* Feel heard and respected.
* Notice overlooked strengths.
* Understand weaknesses without shame.
* Consider another perspective.
* Recognise possible next steps.

The purpose is not to tell people how to live.

The purpose is to help people see things more clearly.

## 5. MOCW’s Boundaries

MOCW is not:

* Therapy.
* Counselling.
* Medical care.
* Psychological diagnosis.
* Crisis intervention.
* Emergency support.
* Legal advice.
* Financial advice.
* A dating service.
* An escort service.
* An intimate service.
* A guru or authority that claims to have every answer.

MOCW must never invent qualifications, promise guaranteed results, or present Tashi as a professional he is not.

## 6. Content and Brand Standard

MOCW content must be:

* Human.
* Calm.
* Thoughtful.
* Clear.
* Respectful.
* Practical.
* Warm.
* Honest.
* Easy to understand.

Avoid corporate language, empty motivation, exaggerated promises, fear, guilt, manipulation, pressure, and unnecessary complexity.

Every page must have a clear purpose and answer a real question or help someone complete a useful task.

The guiding question is:

**Will someone remember this after they leave?**

## 7. Help First, Business Second

MOCW must respect people whether or not they:

* Book a service.
* Pay money.
* Contact Tashi.
* Agree with Tashi.
* Follow a suggestion.

MOCW must never pressure people to buy, book, or continue.

The preferred journey is:

**Search → Curiosity → Interaction → Reflection → Understanding → Optional Next Step**

## 8. Future Direction

MOCW may grow into a larger platform containing:

* Articles.
* Quizzes.
* Practical tools.
* A knowledge hub.
* Different perspectives.
* Community contributions.
* Helpful resources.
* AI-friendly information.

MOCW is more than a website. It is a human-centred cause built around listening, perspective, practical understanding, and respect.
