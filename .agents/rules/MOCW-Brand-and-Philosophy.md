# MOCW Brand and Philosophy Rules

## 1. Brand Identity

Always use the identity:

**My One Cent Worth (MOCW) — Someone Who Listens.**

MOCW should feel human, thoughtful, calm, respectful, practical, and trustworthy.

MOCW is not a corporate brand, therapy service, guru service, dating service, escort service, or intimate service.

## 2. Tone of Voice

Write in a way that is:

* Clear.
* Simple.
* Warm.
* Thoughtful.
* Honest.
* Patient.
* Respectful.
* Practical.

Use ordinary language whenever possible.

Avoid:

* Corporate clichés.
* Complicated wording.
* Empty motivational phrases.
* Aggressive sales language.
* Exaggerated claims.
* False urgency.
* Fear-based marketing.
* Guilt-based marketing.
* Manipulation.
* Talking down to people.

## 3. Tashi’s Position

Tashi is a person people can speak to.

He listens, asks questions, shares perspectives, and helps people consider situations more clearly.

Do not describe Tashi as:

* A doctor.
* A therapist.
* A counsellor.
* A psychologist.
* A guru.
* A guaranteed problem-solver.
* A person with qualifications that have not been confirmed.

## 4. Main Ideas to Preserve

MOCW should consistently reflect:

* Nothing is absolute.
* Different people may see the same thing differently.
* Understanding should come before judgment.
* Questions are valuable.
* Life is a classroom.
* Learning has no finish line.
* Strengths should be recognised.
* Weaknesses should be understood without shame.
* A perspective is not an instruction.

Use this sequence where appropriate:

**Listen • Think • Test • Grow**

Use these foundations where appropriate:

**Facts. Logical Reasoning. Experience. Perspective.**

## 5. Respect for Visitors

Visitors must never feel:

* Judged.
* Pressured.
* Manipulated.
* Shamed.
* Forced to buy.
* Forced to agree.
* Treated as a problem.

MOCW should help first and sell only through clear, honest, optional invitations.

## 6. Content Quality

Every piece of content must answer:

* Who is this for?
* What question does it answer?
* What may the visitor understand afterward?
* Is the information useful?
* Is the language clear?
* Does it sound like MOCW?
* Will someone remember it?

The goal is meaningful content, not content created only to fill a page.
