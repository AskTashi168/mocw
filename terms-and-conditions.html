1. ABOUT MY ONE CENT WORTH

My One Cent Worth is a personal perspective, conversation, and companion service created by Tashi Khoo.

The purpose of My One Cent Worth is simple:

Sometimes we do not need another lecture, another person telling us what to do, or someone claiming to have all the answers.

Sometimes we simply need someone to listen, understand, and offer another perspective.

My One Cent Worth provides conversations, personal perspectives, independent opinions, practical observations, and companionship based primarily on life experience and real-world experience.

The service is designed to help people think more clearly, explore different perspectives, and consider possible next steps.

My One Cent Worth does not claim to have all the answers.
