WHY I STARTED MY ONE CENT WORTH

After six decades of life, I've learned a few things.

I've experienced success, failure, mistakes, and starting again.

I've met many people and listened to many different stories.

Life has taught me things no textbook could.

I've learned that sometimes people don't need advice — they simply need someone to listen.

A good conversation can sometimes help us see things from another perspective.

That's why I created My One Cent Worth.

I'm not claiming to know everything or have all the answers.

I'm simply sharing what life has taught me, one perspective at a time.

You can listen, think about it, agree, disagree, or simply take your own one cent worth.

WHEN YOU NEED SOMEONE TO TALK TO

Life can become complicated quickly.

You may be facing a difficult decision, an unsolved problem, or something you have carried for a long time.

Talking to someone outside your usual circle can help you see things more clearly.

I’ll listen first, then I’ll offer my honest opinion if you want my perspective.

You don’t have to agree with me or know exactly what you want to talk about.

Sometimes, the first step is simply having someone willing to listen.

WHO I AM & WHO I'M NOT

WHO I AM

A listener.

A rationalist and empiricist.

A real person with real-life experience.

Someone who starts with the facts, uses logical reasoning, learns from experience, and looks for the root cause before forming a perspective.

Someone who can offer an honest perspective when you want one.

WHO I'M NOT

I'm not a guru.

I'm not a doctor.

I'm not a therapist.

I'm not a counsellor.

I'm not here to tell you how to live your life.

And I'm not going to pretend that my perspective is always right.

What I offer is conversation, listening, experience and perspective.

MY PROMISE

• I will listen before I speak, and treat your story with complete confidentiality and respect.

• I will give you my honest perspective when you ask for it — never judging you for seeing things differently.

• I will never pretend to know everything or claim my perspective is the absolute answer — because nothing is absolute.

Sometimes, all a person needs is someone who is genuinely willing to listen. That is what I promise to do.
