WHY I STARTED MY ONE CENT WORTH

After six decades of life, I've learned a few things.

I've experienced success, failure, mistakes, and starting again.

I've met many people and listened to many different stories.

Life has taught me things no textbook could.

I've learned that sometimes people don't need advice — they simply need someone to listen.

A good conversation can sometimes help us see things from another perspective.

That's why I created My One Cent Worth.

I'm not claiming to know everything or have all the answers.

Not because I know everything. Not because I have all the answers.

I'm simply sharing what life has taught me, one perspective at a time.

You can listen, think about it, agree, disagree, or simply take your own one cent worth.

SOMETIMES, YOU JUST NEED SOMEONE TO TALK TO

Life can become complicated very quickly.

A decision you're struggling with.
A problem you can't seem to solve.
Something you've been carrying around for a long time.
Or simply a thought you haven't been able to put into words.

Sometimes talking to someone outside your usual circle can help.

Not because they have a magic answer.

But because saying things out loud can help you hear your own thoughts more clearly.

I'll listen first.

If you want my perspective, I'll give you my honest one.

You don't have to agree with me.

You don't even have to know exactly what you want to talk about.

Sometimes the first step is simply having someone there who is willing to listen.

WHY MY ONE CENT WORTH?

People often say, "Here's my two cents."

I chose one.

Not because one cent is worth less.

Because I want every cent I offer to be worth thinking about.

My One Cent Worth is built around four things:

Facts.
Logical Reasoning.
Experience.
Perspective.

I won't pretend that my view is the only view.

There rarely is one perfect answer to a complicated human situation.

My role is simply to listen, think things through and offer what I genuinely see from my side.

You take what is useful. You decide what is right for you.
