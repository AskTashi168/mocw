About Tashi Khoo

A trusted thinking partner, listener, and advisor through life experience.

A little about the person behind My One Cent Worth.

I am not a therapist, counsellor, coach, or guru.

I am simply someone who has lived through life's highs and lows, made mistakes, learned lessons, experienced successes and failures, and gathered perspectives along the way.

Over the years, friends, family members, colleagues, and even strangers have often come to me to talk. Sometimes they wanted advice. Sometimes they wanted a different perspective. More often than not, they simply wanted someone who would listen without judgment.

That is how My One Cent Worth was born.

My Journey

My life has not followed a straight line. I have experienced success, built and run businesses, made difficult decisions, taken risks, faced failures, and learned that achievements and material success do not automatically answer life's deeper questions.

I have also experienced periods of profound difficulty and loss. Those experiences changed me.

Childhood

Growing up in a modest environment taught me the importance of family, observation, and the quiet presence of people who care.

Career

Entering the working world taught me about organisations, management, responsibility, pressure, and the different ways people respond to challenges.

Business

Building and running businesses meant taking risks, making difficult decisions, dealing with people, cash flow, operations, responsibility, success, and failure.

Success

Achievement brought opportunities and material comfort, but it also taught me that outward success does not automatically answer life's deeper questions.

Failures

Failures, losses, and difficult periods stripped away many assumptions and taught me lessons that success never could.

Depression

I went through a dark and deeply difficult period of my life. It taught me how heavy life can feel when a person is struggling, and how easily invisible burdens can be misunderstood by others.

Recovery

Recovery was not a single turning point. It was a gradual process of rebuilding, reflection, patience, acceptance, and learning to move forward again.

Retirement

Stepping away from much of my earlier commercial life created space for reflection, stillness, and a different way of looking at what matters.

A New Beginning

Over time, I began to realise that my experiences did not have to remain private memories. Some of what I had learned might be useful to someone else who was facing a difficult decision, a personal challenge, or simply needed another perspective.

My One Cent Worth

That realisation led to My One Cent Worth. Not as a place for me to tell people how to live, or as a platform for expert advice—but as a space for thoughtful conversation, practical perspective, honest observation, and genuine human connection.

What I Believe

I do not claim to have all the answers. What I can offer is my one cent worth — my thoughts, experience, observations, perspective, and whatever practical wisdom life has taught me.

I believe facts matter. Logical reasoning matters. Experience matters. And perspective matters.

But I also believe that people and situations are rarely as simple as they first appear.

Sometimes a person does not need to be told what to do. Sometimes they need to think aloud, need another angle, or simply need someone willing to listen.

“Sometimes all we need is someone willing to listen. Someone who can help us see things from another angle. Someone who can remind us that we are not alone.”

What I Hope to Offer

My One Cent Worth is built around a simple idea: A good conversation can sometimes make a difference.

Whether you are facing a difficult decision, navigating a personal challenge, feeling stuck, seeking clarity, or simply wanting to talk things through, I am here to listen and share whatever perspective I can.

No judgment. No pressure. No pretending to have all the answers. Just a genuine conversation between two human beings.

If our conversation helps you move forward with a little more clarity, confidence, understanding, or hope, then my one cent worth has served its purpose.

Sincerely,
Tashi Khoo
