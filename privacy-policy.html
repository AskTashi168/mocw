PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026

1. OUR COMMITMENT TO PRIVACY

At My One Cent Worth (MOCW), we take your privacy seriously.

Whether you contact us for a meaningful conversation, perspective service, business consultation, or one of our in-person companion services, we aim to handle your personal information with care, respect, and discretion.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We only collect and use information that is reasonably necessary to communicate with you, provide our services, process bookings and payments, coordinate visits, and operate our website and business.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

Depending on how you interact with MOCW, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • WhatsApp or WeChat contact details
  • Booking date and time
  • Service selected
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us
  • Payment or transaction information necessary to confirm a booking
  • Information necessary to coordinate an in-person companion visit
  • Basic website information such as device, browser, or usage information where analytics or similar website technologies are used

3. INFORMATION FOR IN-PERSON COMPANION SERVICES

For hospital, nursing home, retirement, or other in-person companion services, we may need limited information to arrange and coordinate the visit.

This may include information such as:

  • The name of the person being visited
  • Hospital, nursing home, or other location
  • Ward or room information where necessary
  • Preferred visiting date and time
  • Relevant coordination or access information

We only use this information for purposes reasonably connected with arranging and providing the requested service.

Please do not provide medical information unless it is genuinely necessary for the coordination or safety of the visit.

4. CONVERSATIONS AND CONFIDENTIALITY

MOCW treats conversations and personal information shared during our services as private and confidential.

We do not intentionally record consultation or companion conversations, and we do not intentionally create transcripts of those conversations, unless you have been informed and have expressly agreed to such recording or transcription.

However, confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in circumstances where disclosure is required by law or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to respond to an immediate and serious threat to a person's life or safety.

We also cannot control the privacy practices of third-party communication platforms such as WhatsApp, WeChat, email providers, scheduling services, or other services that you choose to use to communicate with us.

5. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries
  • Communicate with you about a requested service
  • Arrange and manage bookings
  • Provide consultation or companion services
  • Coordinate in-person visits
  • Process or confirm payments
  • Send important information relating to your booking
  • Respond to customer service requests
  • Maintain appropriate business and transaction records
  • Improve the website and user experience
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting, or administrative requirements
  • Protect MOCW, our clients, and others from fraud, misuse, or security risks

6. PAYMENT INFORMATION

Where online payment is available, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Stripe or another payment service used by MOCW.

MOCW does not intentionally store your full credit or debit card number or security code on our own website or systems when payment is processed by a third-party payment provider.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

For PayNow or other payment methods, MOCW may retain reasonable transaction information needed to verify and maintain payment records.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

MOCW may use third-party services to help operate the website and provide our services.

These may include:

  • Payment providers such as Stripe
  • Scheduling or booking services such as Calendly
  • Communication services such as WhatsApp or WeChat
  • Website hosting providers
  • Website analytics or security services, where applicable

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Where practical, we aim to use reputable service providers and only share information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

8. COOKIES AND WEBSITE TECHNOLOGIES

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic website functionality, security, analytics, or improving the user experience.

Cookies are small files or pieces of information stored on your device by a website.

Where cookies or analytics are used, you may be able to control or restrict them through your browser settings.

Some website functions may not work properly if certain cookies are disabled.

9. DATA SECURITY

MOCW takes reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, copying, modification, disposal, or similar risks.

However, no website, electronic communication, or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If we become aware of a data breach that requires notification under applicable law, we will take the appropriate steps required by law.

10. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining business and transaction records, resolving disputes, meeting legal or accounting requirements, and protecting our legitimate business interests.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy, or anonymise it where appropriate.

11. DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION

MOCW does not sell or rent your personal information.

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary:

  • To provide a service you have requested
  • To process a payment or booking
  • To coordinate an in-person visit
  • To service providers assisting us in operating MOCW
  • Where you have given consent
  • Where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law
  • Where necessary to protect the safety of a person or respond to an immediate and serious threat

12. INFORMATION ABOUT OTHER PEOPLE

If you provide MOCW with personal information about another person, such as a family member or person you are arranging a companion visit for, you should have the appropriate authority or permission to provide that information.

Please only provide information that is reasonably necessary for the requested service.

13. OVERSEAS SERVICE PROVIDERS

Some third-party service providers used by MOCW may store or process information outside Singapore.

Where personal information is transferred outside Singapore, MOCW will take reasonable steps to ensure that the information receives a standard of protection that is comparable to the protection required under applicable Singapore data protection requirements.

14. YOUR RIGHTS AND REQUESTS

Subject to applicable law, you may contact MOCW to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you
  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Ask questions about how your information is collected or used
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

Some requests may be subject to legal or practical limitations.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

15. WITHDRAWING CONSENT

Where we rely on your consent to collect, use, or disclose personal information, you may withdraw that consent by contacting us.

Please understand that withdrawing consent may affect our ability to provide certain services or respond to certain requests.

We will explain any relevant consequences where appropriate.

16. CHILDREN

MOCW's services are intended primarily for adults.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing services without appropriate consent or involvement from a parent, guardian, or other authorised person.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided to us improperly, please contact us.

17. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

MOCW may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.

18. CONTACT US

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, how we handle personal information, or wish to make a privacy-related request, please contact us.
