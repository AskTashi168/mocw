Before You Reach Out

If you're wondering whether your situation is important enough to talk about, the answer is yes.

You don't need to have a major life crisis. You don't need to have all your thoughts organised. You don't need to know exactly what to say.

Whether you're facing a difficult decision, feeling stuck, needing a different perspective, or simply wanting someone to listen, you're welcome to reach out. Our conversation doesn't have to solve everything. Sometimes the first step is simply talking things through.

No judgment. No pressure. Just a genuine conversation between two human beings.

Please Note: To protect against unsolicited spam calls, I do not answer incoming phone calls from unknown numbers. Please drop me a WhatsApp message or fill out the form first, and we can schedule a time to talk.

"Remember: You are not alone."

Sincerely,

— Tashi Khoo

What Happens Next?

  1. Send me a message using the form below or WhatsApp.
  2. Tell me as much or as little as you'd like about your situation.
  3. I'll read your thoughts and get back to you as soon as I can.
  4. We'll arrange a consultation conversation if it feels right for both of us.
